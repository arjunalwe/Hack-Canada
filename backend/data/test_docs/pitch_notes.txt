Slide 1: Title
- Working name: "AgriSense" or "CropVision" — need to decide
- Tagline brainstorm: "See disease before it sees you" / "The future of farming is invisible"

Slide 2: Problem
- $220B in annual crop losses globally from plant diseases
- Farmers detect disease too late — by the time you see brown spots, the damage is done
- Current solutions: walk the field (expensive, slow), satellite imagery (too low resolution)
- In Canada specifically: $7.2B in crop losses per year
- Climate change is making disease prediction harder — new patterns, new pathogens

Slide 3: Solution
- Drone-based hyperspectral imaging + AI
- We can detect diseases 5-14 DAYS before any symptoms are visible
- Patent-pending spectral fingerprinting technology
- Think of it as "thermal vision for crops" but way more sophisticated

Slide 4: Technology
- SpectralFormer model: 96.3% accuracy, runs on edge devices
- 2.4M image training dataset (largest of its kind)
- Real-time processing: 15 hectares per hour
- Patent filed: CA 2024/0078432
